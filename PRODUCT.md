## Platform

## Users
- **Corporate buyers (priority growth audience):** office managers, HR, executive assistants and event leads at Halifax companies, firms and institutions. They are gathering quotes for holiday parties, client events and team celebrations, often for a boss, often before they know hours or package. They need a number for budget approval, proof of licensing and insurance, and a PO-friendly invoice.
- **Couples and wedding planners:** the most common booking type today.
- **Private party hosts, festivals and public events.**

## Product Purpose
Golden Service Mobile Bar & Catering is a licensed mobile bar setup (never a trailer or food truck) for indoor and outdoor events in Halifax and across Nova Scotia. It supplies the alcohol, licensing, staff, mixers, ice, garnishes, glassware and debit machines, sets up before guests arrive and tears down after. Success on the site is a completed quote request.

## Positioning
One vendor handles the whole bar: licence, staff, stock, setup, teardown and one itemized invoice. The strongest fit for corporate work is companies hosting in their own space or at unlicensed venues (offices, coworking spaces, galleries, museums, warehouses), where licensing and host liability are the buyer's worry.

## Operating Context
- Quotes are requested through a Formspree form (`https://formspree.io/f/mdapozvl`) and answered by Tristan by email.
- Bartenders are hired per event; December Friday capacity is 10+ staff.
- Special event liquor licences are applied for on the client's behalf at no extra charge.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Static HTML site on GitHub Pages behind Cloudflare, one `index.html` per page, inline CSS/JS.
- Packages: Open Bar, Cash Bar, Drink Tickets (hybrid). Light catering trays at $75 each.
- Corporate payment terms: deposit holds the date; balance invoiced net 15 after the event. PO and EFT accepted.
- Liability insurance: $5M. Certificate of insurance on request.
- All bartenders are Serve Right (responsible beverage service) certified.
- Holiday pricing (decided 2026-09-29): no per-tier prices on the site. One public anchor only: "Office holiday parties typically start under $1,000." Exact pricing goes out by email. The hero sample quote ($950 for 40 guests, 2 hrs) stays as an illustrative example.

## Brand Commitments
- Name: Golden Service Mobile Bar & Catering. Line: "The Golden Standard".
- Voice: direct, warm, efficient, premium without being stuffy. One-page mentality.
- Site copy uses periods or rewording instead of em dashes.

## Product Principles
1. Remove work from the buyer: every claim should answer "what do I not have to do?"
2. Real numbers over adjectives: prices, coverage, capacity, response time.
3. Never fabricate proof, scarcity, testimonials or client names.
4. Corporate buyers never have to wade through wedding content to act.
