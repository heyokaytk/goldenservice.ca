How far ahead should we book a holiday party?

As soon as you have a date. December Fridays book first, and a deposit is what holds your spot.

Can you set up in our office?

Yes. Most office events happen right in the boardroom or lobby. We bring the bar, ice, glassware and debit machines, and leave the space the way we found it.

Do we need to buy the alcohol?

No. We supply beer, wine, spirits, ciders and non-alcoholic options, plus the licence, so there's nothing to buy, chill or haul.

Do you offer non-alcoholic options?

Yes. Every bar includes non-alcoholic choices, and we can make mocktail versions of your signature cocktails so everyone gets the same drink.
