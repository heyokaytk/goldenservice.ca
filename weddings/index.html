Wedding bar questions, answered.

Something else on your mind? Call 902-497-0006 or ask in your quote request.

Can we bring our own alcohol?

No. We supply all the alcohol along with the licence, bartenders, ice and garnishes. One vendor runs the whole bar, so there's nothing for you to buy, chill or haul.

Do you travel outside Halifax?

Yes, anywhere in Nova Scotia. We work weddings from the South Shore to the Annapolis Valley and the North Shore. Outside Halifax, one flat travel fee covers our team's travel and accommodations, and it's listed on your quote.

Who handles the liquor licence?

We do. If your venue needs a special event liquor licence, we apply for it on your behalf at no extra charge.

How many bartenders will we have?

Every wedding gets two professional, Serve Right certified bartenders. Bigger guest lists get more, and we'll tell you how many on your quote.

How do we hold our date?

A non-refundable deposit holds your date. For open bar packages, the balance is due 60 days before the wedding.

Can we have a signature cocktail?

Yes. Tell us what you two love to drink and we'll build a signature cocktail into your bar menu.

Do you provide food?

We offer light fare trays: fruit, veggie and cheese, $75 each, each serving 15 to 20 guests. They're great for cocktail hour or late night. Your dinner itself comes from your caterer or venue.
